Model notes & project scope +

01 / What is predicted

The estimated chance that each team starts a shot within the next 10 seconds. These are model outputs supplied by Abderrahim, not a live feed, possession share, xG or win probabilities.

02 / What is illustrated

Height = 1.5 × the sum of two fixed Gaussian shapes weighted by team probabilities. Team colours blend by local contribution. Wave positions are decorative, not learned locations; the same height scale is used throughout.

03 / Coverage & source

Event data: StatsBomb Open Data. Trained model export by Abderrahim. Imported files are read locally in your browser and are not uploaded. Neither probabilities nor model weights are recalculated here.
